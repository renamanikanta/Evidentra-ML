Simulate a changing feature distribution and calculate Population Stability Index (PSI) against a synthetic reference population.

Increasing shift moves the candidate distribution away from the reference distribution. PSI is a monitoring heuristic, not direct proof that accuracy declined.

Interpreting PSI

Below 0.10 — relatively stable

0.10 to 0.25 — investigate

0.25 and above — high shift
